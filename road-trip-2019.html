Road trip 2019.

San Marcos to Paris, Texas, the long way around. Nine days through Texas, New Mexico, Arizona, Utah and Colorado.

Nine days and a lot of desert. I drove straight west out of San Marcos to the Guadalupe Mountains, went down into Carlsbad Caverns, out onto White Sands, and up to the Grand Canyon and Zion.

Then east into Colorado for Mesa Verde, and home across Texas.
