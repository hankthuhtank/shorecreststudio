Astro.

My adventure buddy.

I adopted Astro in 2015, shortly after my dad passed away. He filled a huge void in my heart and became the perfect adventure buddy.

We went a lot of places together. The trails, the long drives, and the quiet days at home are all part of his story.

Astro passed away on December 31, 2024, at twelve years old. This part of Shorecrest is for him.
