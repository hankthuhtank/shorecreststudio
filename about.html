Helal Safi.

I like being out there. These are the places that made me stop and take a picture.

In 2019, I packed everything I had, took my dog, and hit the road for nine days. No real plan. We drove through the Southwest, stopped at the Grand Canyon, and came home through Colorado.

In 2026, I went back out for forty-five days. That trip took more planning, but the point was the same: get out of the routine and see what was out there. I went from Oklahoma to the Pacific coast, up through Washington, and back through the Mountain West.

The photos are what I brought back. Coastlines, mountains, waterfalls, animals, and whatever else caught my eye. The maps show the roads I actually drove.

Shorecrest

I built Shorecrest as a place for my photography. The name covers both ends of what I like shooting: the shore and the mountains. It also reflects what a lot of us go through: hitting rock bottom, climbing back up, and enjoying the view along the way. Selected photos are available as canvas prints, mugs, and puzzles at shorecrest.store. The clothing sticks to the logo.

What I shoot with

The 2026 photos were taken on an iPhone 17 Pro Max. The 2019 photos came from an earlier phone.
