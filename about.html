I'm Helal Safi.

I take photographs on long drives, mostly with the phone in my pocket.

Most of what's here came from two trips. The first was nine days across Texas, New Mexico, Arizona, Utah and Colorado in 2019. The second was forty-five days, from a cabin in Broken Bow, Oklahoma, out to the Pacific, up to my brother's place in Washington, and home the long way through Glacier, Yellowstone and the Rockies.

I have a massive appreciation for these places. This is where I keep them, along with the actual roads that connected them.

Shorecrest

The name comes from the shore, the lowest ground there is, and the crest, the top of the ridge. Shorecrest is for anyone who's tired of the same routine, and for anyone who hit bottom and found their way back up, one view at a time.

Photographs from the forty-five days also live on as canvas prints, shirts, mugs, puzzles and hats at shorecrest.store.

The camera

Every photograph from the forty-five days was taken on an iPhone 17 Pro Max. The 2019 photographs came from an earlier phone.
