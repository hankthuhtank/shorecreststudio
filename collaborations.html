Shorecrest is my photography from the road: coastlines, mountains, and the places in between. I’m open to working with outdoor and travel brands whose products fit the way I spend time outside.

Building a life on the road.

I’m moving my work into the digital world through SafiSolutions so I can spend the next few years living and working on the road. More time out there means more places to explore and more work to add to Shorecrest.

I spent more than ten years in sales and management before taking this direction. I still care about the same things: understanding what a business needs, communicating clearly, and following through.

Camping, hiking, and fishing are already part of my life. I’d love to put outdoor products to use in those settings and make content around the experience.
