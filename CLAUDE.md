# Working rules for this repo

- Work directly on `main`. Do not create branches or open pull requests unless the owner asks. Pushing to `main` deploys shorecrest.studio.
- Plain HTML/CSS/JS with no build step to serve. `assets/js/data.js` and `assets/js/geo.js` are generated by `tools/build.py` from `tools/trips.json` and the photo notes. Edit the sources and rebuild; never hand-edit the generated files.
- Road routes are cached in `tools/.route-cache.json`. Keep the cache committed so rebuilds don't hit the OSRM server, and keep the OpenStreetMap credit on every map.
- Trip facts come from the owner's own itineraries. Don't invent dates, nights, roads or places. Ask when a detail is unknown.

## Style rules from the owner

- Headings use Fraunces; body text uses Safi (the photographer's handwriting). Keep both.
- No eyebrow labels above headings, no small uppercase letter-spaced or monospace labels, no "big number + label" stat rows, no icon feature grids. Write counts and labels in normal sentence case.
- Rich motion and transitions are welcome, but nothing overdramatic, no postcard, and no endless scrolling pages.
- Write like a person: plain, specific sentences in the owner's first-person voice. Avoid stock marketing phrases.
