Forty-five days.

Broken Bow, Oklahoma, to Paris, Texas, by way of the Pacific. March 1 to April 14.

I left a cabin in Broken Bow on the morning of March 1 and rolled into Paris, Texas, on April 14.

In between: the desert, the Sierra, Highway 1, a week with my brother in Washington, and the long way home through Glacier, Yellowstone, the Tetons and Colorado.
